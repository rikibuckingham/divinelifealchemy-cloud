# Wild Return — Rebrand Direction

> **Status:** Working concept.  
> **Current public brand/domain:** Divine Life Alchemy.  
> **Working project name:** **Wild Return**.  
> Do not rename the live domain or repository yet.

## Name and domain candidates

Current candidates:

- **wildreturn.earth** — front-runner
- **rootedsoul.earth** — strong alternative

Keep **Return** as a core idea even if the final brand name changes.

## Core purpose

Create an earthy, grounded-but-spiritual platform that supports people in:

- becoming more authentic with themselves and with others
- facing wounds rather than continually avoiding or distracting from them
- exploring intergenerational / family-pattern healing carefully and responsibly
- becoming more regulated, focused and consistent rather than scattered
- reconnecting with nature and the body
- nourishing themselves well, including food and everyday lifestyle
- reconnecting with creativity, music, meaning and soul/purpose
- building a stronger, purposeful and more alive life

The work should encourage **both inner and outer life**.

This is not about endless introspection or withdrawing from life. Inner work should support fuller relationships, creativity, nourishment, healthful living, adventure, service, work, music, community and purposeful action in the world.

## Riki's lived journey

The project should be rooted in lived practice rather than presenting a finished system.

Riki's own ongoing process can be part of the story:

- his recovery / healing journey around addiction and compulsive behaviours
- reducing technology and distraction when they become forms of avoidance
- learning to sit gently with difficult feelings and wounded / younger parts of self
- understanding how dysregulation can contribute to feeling scattered
- developing steadiness, focus and consistency
- nourishment, nature, movement, music and creative expression
- becoming more authentic and empowered in relationships and life

### Positioning boundary

Share lived experience honestly without presenting Riki as clinically treating trauma or guaranteeing healing outcomes.

The public message can be about supporting people to face what they have been avoiding, reconnect with themselves, develop healthier patterns and create meaningful change.

## Practice philosophy

Practices should be developed through lived use and refinement.

They should be:

- gentle rather than harsh
- invitational rather than forced
- consistent rather than perfectionistic
- oriented toward gradually meeting difficult feelings rather than automatically escaping them
- practical enough to use in everyday life
- allowed to evolve as experience shows what genuinely helps

Potential vocabulary:

- **The Daily Return** — daily grounding / reconnection practice
- **Return to Self** — possible programme or journey
- **Wild Return Sessions** — one-to-one sessions
- **Wild Return Music** — music / sound journeys
- **Wild Return Gatherings** — events
- **Wild Return Retreats** — future retreats
- **Wild Return Journal** — articles, reflections and lived journey

## Spirituality, nature and lineage

The tone should sit between grounded and mystical.

Possible themes:

- connection with nature
- sacred places and landscapes
- visiting sacred sites and sharing the experience, history, atmosphere and personal reflections
- honouring ancestral and lineage wisdom while distinguishing personal/spiritual interpretation from established historical fact
- music, contemplation, ritual and meaning
- inspiring people to remember their relationship with land, culture, ancestry and the living world

Riki's future travel, including the dream of offering events in Hawaii, can sit naturally within the wider Wild Return journey.

## Music

Music should not feel like a separate side-project.

It can be one of the core expressions of the work:

- original healing-oriented music
- live music within gatherings and retreats
- sound journeys
- songs emerging from Riki's own process
- music as a bridge between inner reflection and living fully in the outside world

## Platform and community vision

The long-term project can be larger than a personal practitioner website.

It may include other carefully selected people who:

- are genuinely engaged in their own healing / growth journey
- have useful embodied experience or spiritual wisdom to share
- offer courses, programmes, sessions or teachings
- can sell paid courses or services through the platform

The platform should maintain a clear quality and ethics bar so it does not become a generic spiritual marketplace.

## Website transition

Do **not** change the domain yet.

First:

1. Reshape the existing Divine Life Alchemy website around this clearer positioning.
2. Help visitors understand Riki and the lived journey before presenting deeper or more esoteric material.
3. Preserve useful existing content while simplifying language and claims.
4. Build a clearer visual and verbal identity.
5. Develop the first practices through real use rather than inventing a rigid system upfront.
6. Reassess **Wild Return** versus **Rooted Soul** after the first identity and website concepts.

## App direction

A **Divine Life Alchemy Android app is already published on Google Play**. Its current focus is the Divine Life Alchemy radio station and podcasts.

This changes the rebrand plan: the existing Play Store app should normally be **rebranded in place** rather than replaced with a new application.

### Preserve the existing Play Store identity

For the existing published Android app:

- keep its current Android application ID / package name
- keep the existing signing key / Play App Signing identity
- publish the rebrand as an update to the same Play Store app
- change the visible app name, icon, colours, splash/login experience and Play Store listing when the final brand is chosen
- existing users should then receive the rebranded application through the normal update path
- do not create a new package solely because the public-facing brand name changes

The technical package name does **not** need to match the public brand name. It may continue to contain a legacy Divine Life Alchemy identifier internally if changing it would require a new Play Store app.

Creating a new package/application ID would mean creating a separate Play Store application rather than updating the existing one, so that should only be done deliberately if there is a compelling reason.

### Product evolution

The existing radio/podcast functionality should be treated as an asset of the future platform rather than discarded.

Possible evolution:

- **Wild Return Radio / Listen** — live radio and audio stream
- **Podcasts / Conversations**
- **Wild Return Music**
- guided practices / The Daily Return
- journal / reflections
- courses and paid programmes
- events / gatherings / retreats
- eventually selected teachers/practitioners and their content

The exact information architecture should be designed later, but the goal is for the current radio-and-podcast app to grow into the wider Wild Return experience rather than starting again from zero.

### iOS / future platforms

If an iOS version has not yet been published, its production bundle identifier can be chosen once the final brand architecture is settled. Prefer a stable organisation namespace so future public brand changes do not force technical identity changes.


## App experience — gentle return, not more distraction

The future app should actively support the philosophy of the project.

A central function should be **gentle interruption of autopilot and distraction**. The app can remind the user to come back to themselves through small, manageable practices rather than demanding long sessions.

Examples:

- pause and take one conscious breath
- spend 5–10 minutes sitting quietly and checking in
- notice what feeling or sensation is present without forcing it away
- put the phone down and step outside
- reconnect with the body through simple movement or stretching
- eat or drink something nourishing with attention
- listen to a short piece of music or sound journey
- write one honest line in a journal
- ask: “What am I avoiding right now?”
- ask: “What do I actually need?”
- reconnect with one meaningful action for the day

### Design principles

The app should **not** become another compulsive engagement product.

Prefer:

- a small number of intentional notifications
- user-controlled reminder times and frequency
- quiet, low-pressure language
- short practices that can be completed without staying in the app
- encouragement to leave the screen and return to real life
- streaks only if they support consistency without shame or pressure
- no manipulative urgency, infinite feeds or attention-maximising mechanics

A possible core feature name is **The Daily Return**: a configurable daily check-in that helps the user breathe, feel, ground, reflect and choose one purposeful next action.

The aim is not perfect discipline. It is to help a scattered nervous system gradually build **presence, focus, steadiness and consistency** through gentle repetition.
